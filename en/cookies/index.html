Cookies

Information about cookies, temporary browser storage and third-party content on this website.

This website does not currently use first-party analytics or advertising cookies. Some technical functionality and external providers may nevertheless involve browser storage.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored by a website or an external service in your browser. Similar technologies, such as sessionStorage, can remember interface settings temporarily without setting a conventional cookie.

2. What this website uses

TechnologyPurposeDuration
lucia_intro_seen (sessionStorage)Remembers that the opening animation has already played in the current tab session.Until the browser tab or session ends.
Hosting and security logsCloudflare may process technical data and apply security measures to deliver this website.According to the provider's policies.

3. Google Maps and externally hosted images

The homepage includes an embedded Google map. When your browser loads the map, requests are sent to Google, which may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Images hosted on accommodation portals may also connect your browser to those portals' servers. Their own privacy notices apply to such services. Directions can also be opened directly in Google Maps.

4. Analytics and advertising

There is currently no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other advertising tracking script installed on this website. If these features are added later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

5. Managing browser storage

You can review, block or erase cookies and website data in your browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect the display of external content. For more information about personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

For questions about privacy or cookies, email apartmenthouse.lucia@gmail.com. Last updated: 11 November 2026.
