Privacy

How we handle personal information when you browse this website or enquire about accommodation.

We respect the privacy of guests and visitors to Apartmani Lucia. This notice explains what information is processed, for what purposes, and how to exercise your rights.

1. Data controller and contact

The accommodation provider operating as Apartmani Lucia, Marka Marulića 3a, 23210 Biograd na Moru, Croatia, is responsible for handling accommodation enquiries. For privacy questions contact apartmenthouse.lucia@gmail.com or +385 91 240 4046.

2. Information we process

When you enquire, we may process your name, email address, arrival and departure dates, number of guests, apartment preference and any message you provide. If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, we process the information you share. Our hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP address, access time and browser details, for security and delivery of the website.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to enquiries and preparing a booking: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Website security and prevention of misuse: legitimate interests in protecting the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations: if a booking is concluded, certain records may be retained where required by law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4. Enquiries and service providers

You can contact us by email, phone or through the website form once activated. The form is prepared to use Web3Forms; when enabled, submitted details will be transmitted through this provider to the host's email. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and email communication uses an email service provider. These providers may process the data needed to operate and secure their services.

5. External content and links

The website may display photographs hosted on accommodation portals and an embedded Google map. Loading such external content may connect your browser to third-party servers, which may process technical data or use their own storage technologies. Opening Google Maps, Booking.com or WhatsApp links takes you to those providers, whose privacy policies then apply. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. Retention

Enquiry information is kept as long as reasonably needed to respond and follow up, and for longer only when a reservation or legal retention obligation applies. Hosting logs follow the hosting provider's retention policies. We do not sell visitor data or use it for advertising profiling.

7. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, object to certain processing or request data portability. Contact apartmenthouse.lucia@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Croatian supervisory authority, the Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

8. Security and updates

We apply reasonable technical and organisational security measures. This notice may be updated if website features or processing practices change. Last updated: 11 November 2026.
